# Quran Companion – Mushaf pages

Page images (Madinah Mushaf, 1024 px wide, 604 pages) downloaded on demand by the
Quran Companion Android app, so the app itself stays small. The files are attached
to the `mushaf-1024-v1` release; the app checks each one against
`mushaf-1024.sha256` before using it.

## Ownership and terms

- The Madinah Mushaf belongs to the **King Fahd Glorious Qur'an Printing Complex**
  (qurancomplex.gov.sa). Its digital-Mushaf copyright page states that the digital
  Mus'haf al-Madinah "can be used for free" in personal, business, governmental and
  institutional work, including digital publishing, websites and software.
- These images were rendered from the Complex's Madinah fonts by the open-source
  [quran.com images project](https://github.com/quran/quran.com-images), as
  distributed by [Quran for Android](https://github.com/quran/quran_android).
- The images are unmodified and are served here only for a free, ad-free app, in
  line with Quran for Android's request that its data be used for non-profit purposes.
